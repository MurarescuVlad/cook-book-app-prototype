Behavior notes

  • No backend: favorites and the shopping list persist in localStorage; all recipe data comes from the TheMealDB API.
  • “Recipe of the day” is a random recipe, fetched on every visit to the home page.
  • Shopping-list items are grouped by the recipe they came from; the same ingredient added from two recipes stays in two groups.
  • Header badges read the same stores, so they update live on every page.
  • The category filter is client-side only; the search page queries the API by recipe name.
  • Page titles follow “Title – CookBook” (e.g. “Pasta alla carbonara – CookBook”).
  • Favorite state is shared: the heart on the recipe card, the recipe page button and the favorites page all reflect one store.

Team bonus — behavior notes Team

  • The plan lives in its own global store (e.g. Zustand) and persists in localStorage, like favorites and the shopping list.
  • A slot stores a reference to one recipe. Either save the data the planner needs (id, name, image, ingredients) when adding, or fetch with lookup.php when needed — then show loading and error states.
  • Header badge, recipe-page “Planned for”, summary counters and the ingredients view are all derived from the same planner store, so they update live.
  • “Add week to shopping list” reuses the shopping-list store and its “already in the list” check — the shopping list keeps grouping by recipe.
  • Page title: “Meal Planner – CookBook”.
